Privacy Policy

This notice explains how Nymix handles information when you use its music, profile, messaging, and Watch Together features.

Information we handle

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you use email and password, Nymix stores a password hash, not your plain-text password. If you sign in with Google, Nymix receives your verified email, name, and Google account identifier; Nymix does not receive your Google password.
  • Profile and content: information you choose to add, such as a profile photo, bio, profile photos and captions, likes, and messages. Messages may include text, voice recordings, or references to a shared profile photo.
  • Service activity: friend requests and relationships, music likes and play history, and information needed to run chats, listening or watch rooms, invitations, games, and calls.
  • Browser storage: after sign-in, an authentication token is kept in your browser's local storage so you can remain signed in. You can sign out to remove it from that browser.

How we use information

We use this information to create and secure accounts, provide the features you request, show your profile and content to other signed-in members, deliver messages and invitations, and maintain the service. Google sign-in information is used to authenticate you and associate your Nymix account with your Google account. Nymix does not request access to your Google Drive or YouTube account.

Who can see or receive information

Information in your profile and community photos can be seen by signed-in Nymix members. Private messages are available to the chat participants, and room content is available to room members or invitees. Only share content you are comfortable sharing with those people.

Nymix uses Google for sign-in, Vercel for production hosting, and the database configured for the service to operate and store app data. YouTube services may be used for music search and embedded media. Google and YouTube may process information under their own privacy policies when you use their sign-in or media features. Audio calls and watch-room sharing use browser WebRTC; Nymix handles connection setup, while media is exchanged between participants or through a configured relay. Nymix does not record call audio or video.

Storage and retention

Production account and app data is stored in the service database. During local development, data is stored in local files. We keep information while it is needed to operate the service. Some room and connection data is temporary, while account details, profiles, and conversation history may persist. Shared messages may remain available to other participants.

Your choices and requests

You can update your profile and remove profile photos in the app. Nymix does not currently provide a self-service account-deletion control. To ask for account deletion or to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, contact us using the address below. We may ask you to verify your account before handling a request. Some information may need to be retained where required for legitimate service, safety, or legal purposes.

Security

Nymix uses measures such as password hashing and authenticated access to help protect information. No online service can guarantee complete security.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the service or its data practices change. The current version will be published on this page.
